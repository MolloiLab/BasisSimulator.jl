Scatter in Water Cylinders

Before any scatter model can be trusted it has to reproduce what has been measured. This notebook fires a CT fan beam through water cylinders from 20 to 50 cm in Geant4, every photon followed through the patient, and scores every scattering on the detector through its anti-scatter grid by forced detection (notebook 10 explains it), and asks four things:

  1. How does scatter grow with patient size, with no grid, a 1-D grid and a 2-D grid?

  2. Does it match the literature: the wide-cone measurements of Endo et al. on a Toshiba 256-row prototype, the scatter fractions of Kyriakou and Kalender on a Siemens Definition, and the 2-D against 1-D grid reductions of Melnyk et al.?

  3. Is the grid model right: the probability that a photon at an angle gets through a grid, against Geant4 transporting it through the tungsten walls?

  4. How far is BasisSimulator v0.15.1's analytic model (one convolution scaled by one diameter, which v0.16 replaced with the library) from all of this?

Every answer is a number with a target; the notebook ends with its scorecard.

How Scatter Reaches the Detector

A photon from the focal spot either crosses the patient untouched (the primary, which carries the image) or is deflected (Compton or Rayleigh) and, if it still leaves the patient, may reach the detector somewhere else (the scatter). The anti-scatter grid's walls point at the focal spot: a primary passes between them, a scattered photon arriving at an angle mostly hits one. The scatter-to-primary ratio (SPR) at a detector element is the measure of how much the image is contaminated.

primary_in_bin (generic function with 1 method)
"""
The primary per photon in the central detector bin of `s`, exactly: the bin's share of the fan's
photons, of the beam's rows, and Beer–Lambert through `length_mm` of material with attenuation `μ`.
"""
function primary_in_bin(s, length_mm, μ, collimation_mm)
    e, w = spectrum
    weights = w ./ sum(w)
    width = collimation_mm * s.source_to_detector_mm / s.source_to_isocenter_mm
    row_share = min(s.row_bin_mm, width) / width
    column_share = s.column_bin_deg / s.fan_deg
    detected = sum(
        wk * exp(-μ[clamp(round(Int, Ek), 1, 150)] * length_mm) * s.efficiency[clamp(round(Int, Ek), 1, 150)] * Ek
            for (Ek, wk) in zip(e, weights)
    )
    return column_share * row_share * detected
end

function fan_scan(scanners_, diameter_mm, material, collimation_mm; n = 1_000_000, seed = 1)

    )

end

"""
BasisSimulator v0.15.1's SPR at the central detector element, for a water cylinder of
`diameter_mm` in `scanner`'s beam: its scatter field from the polychromatic projection, its
energy weighting, over the primary.
"""
function bs_spr(scanner, diameter_mm)
    e, w = spectrum
    μ = geant4_mu[:Water]
    cols, rows = scanner.detector_cols, scanner.detector_rows
    Δγ = scanner.detector_col_size / scanner.source_to_isocenter
    R = diameter_mm / 2
    sinogram = zeros(Float64, cols, rows, 1)
    for i in 1:cols
        b = scanner.source_to_isocenter * sin((i - (cols + 1) / 2) * Δγ)
        L = abs(b) < R ? 2 * sqrt(R^2 - b^2) : 0.0
        transmitted = sum(wk * exp(-μ[clamp(round(Int, Ek), 1, 150)] * L) for (Ek, wk) in zip(e, w)) / sum(w)
        sinogram[i, :, 1] .= -log(transmitted)
    end
    scatter = MC.basissimulator_v0_15_1_scatter(
        sinogram;
        source_to_isocenter_mm = scanner.source_to_isocenter,
        source_to_detector_mm = scanner.source_to_detector,
        detector_col_size_mm = scanner.detector_col_size,
        phantom_diameter_cm = diameter_mm / 10,
        energies = e,
        weights = w,
    )
    c, r = (cols + 1) ÷ 2, (rows + 1) ÷ 2
    return scatter[c, r, 1] / exp(-sinogram[c, r, 1])
end

"""
The scatter-to-primary ratio at the centre of `name`'s detector, from the library, for a water
cylinder of `diameter_mm` in a beam of `collimation_mm`; with `bowtie`, every ray's photons are
first filtered by the bowtie's aluminium at its angle (fewer, and harder, toward the edges).
"""
function library_central_spr(name, diameter_mm, collimation_mm; bowtie_on)
    s = library.scanners[findfirst(x -> x.name == name, library.scanners)]
    e, w = spectrum
    μ_al = [MC.linear_attenuation(MC.DetectorMaterial(:Al), Float64(E)) for E in 1:150]
    cols = MC.column_edges(s)
    R = diameter_mm / 2
    S, P = 0.0, 0.0
    for γ in (cols[1] + 0.05):0.1:cols[end]
        abs(γ) <= s.fan_deg / 2 || continue
        b = s.source_to_isocenter_mm * sind(γ)
        abs(b) < R || continue
        t = bowtie_on ? bowtie.aluminium_mm[argmin(abs.(bowtie.angles_deg .- γ))] : 0.0
        filtered = [wk * exp(-μ_al[clamp(round(Int, Ek), 1, 150)] * t) for (Ek, wk) in zip(e, w)]
        ray = (angle_deg = γ, wet_mm = 2 * sqrt(R^2 - b^2), weight = 0.1 / s.fan_deg * sum(filtered) / sum(w))
        scan = MC.library_scan(
            library,
            name,
            [ray];
            collimation_mm,
            spectrum = (e, filtered),
            ellipse = (thickness_mm = diameter_mm, width_mm = diameter_mm, centre_mm = 0.0),
        )
        c, r = (size(scan.scatter, 1) + 1) ÷ 2, (size(scan.scatter, 2) + 1) ÷ 2
        S += scan.scatter[c, r]
        P += scan.primary[c, r]
    end
    return S / P
end

4. The Grid Model Against Geant4

The scatter scoring lets a photon through a grid with probability 1 − H tan α / opening per direction of walls. Geant4 checks it: photons at an angle α across the fan land uniformly over one cell of the Force's 2-D grid (10 mm tall, 80 µm of tungsten), on a 2 mm tungsten absorber below it; the fraction absorbed, relative to straight-down, is the grid's transmission.
